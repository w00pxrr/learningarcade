RIP

We are sorry to inform you that our learningarcade has been shut down. its source is now published at: https://github.com/w00pxrr/learningarcade

After over 2 months of being popular at my school, i have decided to pull the plug on Learningarcade, as i am just a kid, and i have a life outside of developing learningarcade.

While I am proud of what we have achieved with Learningarcade, I have come to realize that maintaining and supporting the platform requires a significant amount of time and effort. As a student, I need to prioritize my studies and personal life, and unfortunately, I cannot continue to dedicate the necessary resources to keep Learningarcade running.

I want to express my sincere gratitude to all of you who have supported Learningarcade and made it a success. Your enthusiasm and feedback have been invaluable, and I am truly grateful for the community we have built together. I hope that the source code will allow others to learn from our work and perhaps even continue to develop the platform in the future.

Also, the vercel data breach got the server running the website hacked, and all enviroment variables and api keys leaked. there was also dying viewing, as we used to get 1k+ views per day, now we get 20.
